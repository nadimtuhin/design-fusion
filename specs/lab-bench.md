# Lab bench (proposed by Opus)

Graph-paper white, a signal-green accent and monospace data text. Playful and engineered, with no purple AI gradient. Preview: `themes/lab-bench.html`.

- Tokens: --bg #FAFAF7, --surface #FFFFFF, --grid #ECEBE4 (1px lines, 24px cell), --ink #0E1116, --ink-2 #4A5160, --line #DAD8CF, --accent #00C46A (fills and focus), --accent-ink #007A42 (text and links), --pop #FF5A1F (large text only). Grades: pass #0F8A4B, warn #A86200, fail #C8283C.
- Type: display `ui-rounded, "SF Pro Rounded", system-ui` at 800, -0.03em. Data `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo`. Scale 13/15/17/22/32/clamp(40px,7vw,76px).
- Hero: h1 left-aligned on graph paper, a hand-drawn green squiggle under the key words, and a tilted orange "free · no signup" sticker.
- Scan card: 2px ink border with a hard `6px 6px 0` ink shadow. Pill toggle with a sliding green thumb. Mono `https://` prefix chip. Ink button that turns green on hover.
- Results: a 160px ring with a mono number. The grade is a rotated red teacher's stamp. Family cards get a 4px top border (ink, green, orange). Failing rows get a 3px red left rail.
- Motion: a typewriter log with a blinking cursor while scanning; rings count up over 900ms, then the stamp squashes in; buttons press 2px into their shadow.
- Cut: glows, gradients, glassmorphism, and any soft shadow.
